# Briefing: new mandatory reporting field

**Decision needed:** how to deliver the Ministry's new reporting field, requested at all 1,500 facilities within two weeks.

## The issue

Adding a field sounds small. Here it means three changes, each of which must work at every facility:

1. **Updating every facility's database.** Some facilities are offline for days, so updates reach them slowly.
2. **Changing how facilities send data nationally.** Older versions of CareLink cannot read the new format, so facilities not yet updated could stop sending data altogether.
3. **A security review** of a new link to an outside system, which protects patient data.

Engineering estimates six weeks to do this safely.

## The risk of rushing

Updates are installed while clinics are open. A faulty one could stop clinics recording visits, or silently stop data reaching national level. Such failures are often noticed only weeks later, as gaps in national figures.

## Options

**A. All facilities in two weeks.** Meets the date on paper, but testing is cut short and the security review rushed or skipped. High risk of disrupting care and losing data; repairing offline facilities could take longer than six weeks.

**B. Phased delivery (recommended).** In week 2 the field goes live at about 400 well-connected facilities. All others record the same information on a temporary form, so nothing is lost. Data goes to the new outside system only after its security review. The remaining facilities are updated in groups during weeks 3 to 6.

**C. All facilities after six weeks.** Lowest technical risk, but no data on the new field for six weeks, which may conflict with the Ministry's reporting obligations.

## Recommendation

**Option B.** It starts collecting data within the requested two weeks while protecting patient care and patient data. It requires two agreements:

- the Ministry accepts full national coverage at week 6;
- programme teams support the temporary form in the meantime.

## Consequences at a glance

| Option | Data from week 2 | Risk to patient care | Complete by |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Yes, if nothing fails | High | Unpredictable |
| B | Partly, plus temporary forms | Low | Week 6 |
| C | No | Lowest | Week 6 |
